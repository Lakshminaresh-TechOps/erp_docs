# Why Software Pricing Should Encourage Team Collaboration, Not Penalize It

In the modern SaaS ecosystem, per-seat pricing has become the default monetization model. On paper, it sounds reasonable: pay for what you use. In reality, it acts as a punitive tax on company growth.

---

## 1. The Per-Seat Dilemma

Consider a typical 20-person distribution company:
- 2 founders / executives
- 3 accountants and billing clerks
- 5 sales representatives
- 8 warehouse and packing staff
- 2 customer support agents

Under conventional ERP pricing, every additional login can increase the monthly bill even when that person only needs simple, occasional access.

Faced with these punitive numbers, small business owners make dangerous compromises:
1. **Account Sharing:** 8 warehouse workers share a single login named `warehouse@company.com`. When an inventory discrepancy occurs, there is zero audit trail to identify who recorded the adjustment.
2. **Exclusion of Frontline Staff:** Staff members who could benefit from viewing real-time stock levels or delivery notes are denied access entirely and forced back into paper logs or WhatsApp messages.
3. **Friction at Every Hire:** Every new hire becomes a budgetary headache because of the software tax attached to their desk.

---

## 2. A Better Model: Value-Based Bundled Tiers

At VAP ERP, we asked a fundamental engineering question:  
*Should a warehouse clerk checking a stock balance be priced the same way as a finance lead running month-end reports?*

Of course not. Everyday operational access should be priced around business value, not around a rigid per-seat habit.

Charging the same per-user fee for every role often punishes the teams that need accountability the most.

### The VAP ERP Approach:
- **Generous User Bundles:** Our plans provide practical user limits out of the box so small teams can work with named accounts from the beginning.
- **Encourage Clean Accountability:** Because adding a warehouse operator or junior clerk does not trigger a punitive monthly fee, companies can give every employee their own secure login with granular permissions.
- **Audit Trails That Actually Work:** When every employee has their own account, your business gains true security: two-factor authentication, named activity logs, and strict role segregation.

---

## 3. Technology Built for Real Business Growth

Software should be an operational asset, not an overhead tax. By aligning our pricing model with real operational value and keeping our own operations disciplined, we provide growing businesses with secure ERP tools at a price that leaves capital where it belongs—in your business.
