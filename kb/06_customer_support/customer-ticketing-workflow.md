# Customer Support Ticketing & Issue Resolution

The **Customer Support Desk** module in VAP ERP enables your client service team to centralize incoming complaints, bug reports, and service requests, ensuring no customer issue falls through the cracks.

---

## 1. Creating & Ingesting Support Tickets

Tickets enter the support desk through two primary channels:
1. **Manual Ticket Creation:** An agent receiving a phone call or direct email clicks **+ New Ticket**, selects the customer account, and logs the issue summary.
2. **Customer Portal Submission:** Clients can submit tickets directly through their customer portal, attaching screenshots and setting urgency levels.

---

## 2. Ticket Properties & Lifecycle

Every support ticket tracks key metadata to help your team prioritize:
- **Ticket ID:** Unique reference number (e.g. `#TCK-1082`) shared with the customer for tracking.
- **Customer Account:** Linked to the CRM and Finance directory so agents can immediately view the client's past purchases and subscription status.
- **Priority:** `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, or `Urgent`.
- **Status Stages:**
  - **Open:** Newly created; pending agent review.
  - **In Progress:** Agent actively investigating or troubleshooting.
  - **Waiting on Customer:** Awaiting customer clarification or testing.
  - **Resolved:** Issue fixed; pending customer confirmation.
  - **Closed:** Ticket completed.

---

## 3. Collaborative Resolution & Internal Notes

- **Customer-Facing Replies:** Messages entered in the main response area are visible to the customer and sent to their email.
- **Internal Private Notes:** Agents can toggle **Private Note** (highlighted in yellow) to leave confidential notes for coworkers or escalate technical details to engineering without the customer seeing them.
- **Ticket Assignment:** Transfer ownership of a ticket between team members or departments with one click.

---

## 4. Measuring Support Performance

Managers can review team efficiency from the **Support Dashboard**:
- **First Response Time:** How quickly agents acknowledge new inquiries.
- **Resolution Time:** Total hours elapsed from ticket creation to final resolution.
- **Open Ticket Backlog:** Real-time visibility into unresolved issues organized by priority.
