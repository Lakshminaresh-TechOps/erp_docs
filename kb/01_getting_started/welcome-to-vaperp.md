# Welcome to VAP ERP

VAP ERP is a unified, modern business management platform designed to help growing businesses, traders, and service firms manage daily operations without the complexity or steep costs of traditional enterprise software.

Instead of juggling disjointed spreadsheets, disconnected invoicing apps, and isolated lead trackers, VAP ERP brings your entire business workflow into one connected workspace.

---

## 1. What Makes VAP ERP Different?

- **All-in-One Integration:** When a new lead is won in your CRM, you can convert it to a sales quotation in one click. When an invoice is paid, your inventory adjusts and your accounts update automatically.
- **Predictable Tiered Pricing:** We believe software should help your company grow, not penalize you with multiplying monthly license fees as you hire more team members.
- **Data Privacy by Design:** Your business data resides in dedicated, isolated database boundaries. Your confidential client lists, pricing, and accounting records are never commingled.
- **Instant Cloud Access:** Access your company portal securely from any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile with zero software installation required.

## 2. Core Business Tools at a Glance

The modules visible to each person depend on the workspace subscription and the permissions assigned by an administrator.
If an expected module is missing, ask your workspace administrator to verify both the subscription entitlement and your assigned role before contacting support.

| Module | What It Does for Your Business |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Finance & Invoicing** | Create tax-compliant invoices, record customer payments, generate balance sheets, and track outstanding collections. |
| **Leads & CRM** | Capture inbound customer inquiries automatically from your website, manage sales deals on a visual board, and track conversations. |
| **Inventory & Warehouses** | Monitor real-time stock levels across multiple locations, receive low-stock reorder alerts, and value your inventory. |
| **HR & Team Directory** | Maintain employee profiles, track attendance and leaves, organize departmental structures, and prepare payroll. |
| **Customer Support Desk** | Manage incoming customer inquiries, assign support tickets, track resolution times, and maintain customer history. |
| **School Management (SMS)** | Manage student enrollment, academic terms, guardian contacts, and fee collection schedules. |

## 3. How to Get Started

1. **Sign In to Your Workspace:** Navigate to your organization's unique web address (e.g. `https://yourcompany.vaperp.com`) using your administrator credentials.
2. **Configure Company Settings:** Go to **Admin Center → Company Profile** to set your legal business name, currency, address, and upload your company logo for invoices.
3. **Invite Team Members:** Add your colleagues under **Admin Center → User Management**, assigning them appropriate roles (such as *Finance Manager*, *Sales Representative*, or *Warehouse Operator*).
4. **Explore the Modules:** Use the **App Launcher** in the top navigation bar to switch seamlessly between Finance, CRM, Inventory, and HR.
