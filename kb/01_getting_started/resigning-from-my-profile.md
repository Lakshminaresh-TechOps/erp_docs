# Resigning from My Profile

Everyone resigns in the same place: **My Profile → Resignation**. It works whether or not your organisation uses the HR module, and you do not need access to HR or Admin.

---

## 1. Who Approves Your Resignation

| Your organisation | Who approves |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Does not use HR, or HR has no employee record for you | Your **department manager** (or an organisation admin) |
| Uses HR and HR has an employee record for you | Your **department manager** and your **department HR**, both of them |

Department managers are named by your organisation's admin. Before you submit, the form shows who will approve it.

---

## 2. Submitting a Resignation

1. Open **My Profile** and choose the **Resignation** tab.
2. Under **My resignation**, click **Resign**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Last working day.** It must respect your notice period, unless you ask to waive it.
   - **Reason (optional)**, for example *moving to another city*.
   - **Ask to waive the notice period**, if you want to leave before your notice ends.
   - **Which department are you leaving from?** This is only asked if you belong to more than one department.
4. Click **Submit**.

The notice period is counted **from the day you first submit**. The form tells you the earliest last working day it allows.

You can have only **one open resignation** at a time.

---

## 3. While It Is Waiting

**My resignation** shows:
- the status, **Waiting for approval**;
- each approval step, and who has approved it so far.

**Withdrawing:** while it is waiting or sent back, click **Withdraw**. You can submit a new one later.

---

## 4. When It Is Sent Back

An approver can send your resignation back with a note, for example *"Please give the full notice."*

1. **My resignation** shows **Sent back**, who sent it back and the note.
2. Click **Change and submit again**.
3. Change the last working day, the reason or the notice waiver, then click **Submit again**.

The approvals **start again**. Earlier approvals do not count for the new submission.

---

## 5. When It Is Accepted

- **Without HR:** your resignation shows **Accepted**.
- **With HR:** your resignation shows **Accepted**, and HR now handles your notice period, no-dues checklist and final settlement.

**Your sign-in** keeps working until the **end of your last working day**. It is switched off during the following night. If your organisation needs you for longer, an organisation admin can keep your access until a later day.

---

## 6. If You Approve Resignations

If you are a department manager, a department HR person or an organisation admin, resignations waiting for you appear at the top of the same tab, under **Waiting for my approval**.

1. Click **Review**.
2. Choose one:
   - **Approve.** You can change the **Agreed last working day**. It must respect the notice period, unless the notice is waived.
   - **Send back to the employee.** Write **What should the employee change?** A note is required.
3. Click **Approve** or **Send back**.

Nobody can approve their own resignation.

---

## 7. Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Waiting for approval** | Submitted; waiting for the approvers |
| **Sent back** | Returned to you with a note to change and submit again |
| **Accepted** | Approved; your last working day is agreed |
| **Not accepted** | An older resignation that was decided before the current approval flow |
| **Withdrawn** | You withdrew it |
| **Left** | Your last working day has passed and your sign-in was switched off |

---

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**"I don't see a Resign button."**
You already have an open resignation. Change it or withdraw it under **My resignation**.

**"Can I resign in HR → Resignations?"**
No. HR → Resignations shows **Resign in My Profile**. HR people still see and approve requests there.

**"The form says HR could not be reached."**
Some information comes from the HR module. Wait a moment and open the tab again.

**"My last working day was yesterday and I still need to sign in."**
Ask an organisation admin to keep your access until a later day, before the night after your last working day.
