# Account Security & Two-Factor Authentication

Protecting your sensitive financial records, customer details, and business data is foundational in VAP ERP. We provide multi-layered security controls designed to keep your business safe without slowing down your day-to-day operations.

---

## 1. Setting Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication (also known as Multi-Factor Authentication or MFA) adds an essential second layer of protection beyond your password. When enabled, signing in requires a 6-digit verification code generated by your mobile device.

### Step-by-Step Setup:
1. Sign in to your workspace at `https://yourcompany.vaperp.com`.
2. Click your profile avatar in the top right corner and select **Account Settings**.
3. Under the **Security** tab, find **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** and click **Enable 2FA**.
4. Open an authenticator app on your smartphone (such as **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator**, or **Authy**).
5. Scan the QR code displayed on your computer screen with your phone's camera.
6. Enter the 6-digit code shown in your authenticator app into the verification box on VAP ERP.
7. Click **Confirm & Activate**.
8. **Crucial:** Save the generated **Backup Recovery Codes** in a secure location (e.g. a password manager). These codes allow you to regain access if you lose or replace your mobile device.

## 2. Password Best Practices

- Use a unique passphrase of at least 12 characters combining letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Never share your personal login credentials with coworkers. Every team member should have their own named account so actions can be traced in the audit log.
- If you suspect your password has been compromised, reset it immediately via **Account Settings → Change Password** or use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in screen.

## 3. Role-Based Access Controls (For Administrators)

As a Tenant Administrator, you control exactly what each employee can see and do:
- **Finance Team:** Can view and create invoices and ledgers, but cannot see sensitive HR salary figures or delete system audit logs.
- **Sales Team:** Can view and update CRM leads and create customer quotations, but cannot modify bank reconciliation accounts.
- **Warehouse Staff:** Can adjust stock levels and inspect dispatch shipments, without access to company financial summaries.

To review or modify permissions, navigate to **Admin Center → Roles & Permissions**.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens if an employee loses their phone with their 2FA app?**  
A Tenant Administrator can securely reset the employee's two-factor authentication from the **Admin Center → User Management** console. Once reset, the employee can log in with their password and register a new device.

**Can administrators enforce 2FA for all company employees?**  
Yes. In **Admin Center → Security Policies**, administrators can toggle **Enforce 2FA for All Users**. Once activated, all employees will be prompted to configure their authenticator app on their next login before accessing company data.
