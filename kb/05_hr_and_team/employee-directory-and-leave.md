# Employee Directory & Leave Management

The **HR** module keeps staff records, attendance, leave and resignations in one place. Open it from the **App Launcher**. The sidebar has these pages:

- **Dashboard**
- **Employees**
- **Workforce**
- **Joining soon**
- **Documents**
- **Resignations**
- **Attendance**
- **Leaves**
- **Payroll**
- **Settings**

What you see depends on your HR role. Rights are set in **HR → Settings → Access**.

---

## 1. Employees and Their Profiles

**How people get into HR**

Everyone added in **Admin → Users** is an employee. You do not add employees in HR itself.

1. Add the person in **Admin → Users**.
2. In **HR → Employees**, they appear under **People awaiting enrolment**.
3. Click **Complete profile** and fill in:
   - **Department**
   - **Designation**
   - **Employment type**
   - **Join date**
   - **Work location**
4. Save. The employee starts in **Onboarding**. Nobody enters payroll until HR makes them active and their pay is approved.

People hired through **Recruit** appear first in **HR → Joining soon**.

**Finding and opening an employee**

- **HR → Employees** lists everyone, with their employee code, designation, department and status.
- Use the search box, or filter by status, for example *Active*, *Probation*, *Notice period* or *Relieved*.
- Click a person to open their page. It shows their employment details, documents, onboarding checklist and, after a resignation is accepted, their **No-dues clearance** checklist.

**Departments**

Departments are set up in **Admin → Organisation**. They are used for:
- each employee's department;
- deciding who approves a resignation.

Restrict access to personal addresses, emergency contacts, compensation, and identity records to authorized HR roles with a legitimate business need.

## 2. Attendance

Open **HR → Attendance**. The page shows daily summaries, check-in and check-out, and corrections.

**Employees**

- Click **Check in / out** to record the start or end of your day. You can share your location, but it is not required.
- If you forgot to check in or out, click **Request a correction**, give the time and a reason (for example *forgot to clock out*), and submit it for approval.

**HR and approvers**

- **Mark for employee:** record attendance for someone, for example someone who forgot their phone. It is recorded as entered by HR.
- **Review queue:** check-ins HR must verify, such as those outside the allowed area, flagged locations and missed-punch requests. Approve or reject each one.
- **Worksites:** set the places where staff check in, and the allowed area around each.
- **Everyone on a day:** see the whole team's attendance for one date.

## 3. Leave Requests and Approvals

Leave types and their rules are set in **HR → Settings → Leave policies**.

**Requesting leave (employee)**

1. Open **HR → Leaves**. **My balances** shows how much leave you have available.
2. Click **Request leave**.
3. Choose the **Leave type**, the **From** and **To** dates and, if you like, a **Reason (optional)**.
4. Submit. The days are held from your balance until the request is decided.

Your requests are listed under **My requests**:
- Click **History** to see who has decided so far.
- Click **Cancel** on a request that is still waiting. The hold is released.

**Approving leave (approver)**

1. Open **HR → Leaves**. Requests for you are under **Awaiting my decision**.
2. Open a request. The **Leave decision** window shows the dates and the time asked for.
3. Add a **Comment (optional)**, then click **Approve** or **Reject**.

A leave request can have more than one approval step. Approving the **final** step takes the leave from the balance. An earlier step keeps the hold in place.

**Leave for someone else (HR)**

Click **Create leave for employee**, choose the employee, leave type and dates, then click **Create leave**. It is held until an approver decides.

## 4. Resignations

Resignations are handled on **HR → Resignations**:

- An employee submits a resignation there.
- Their **department manager** and **department HR** approve it at the same time.
- When both have approved, the resignation is accepted and the notice period starts.
- Either of them can send it back to the employee to change and submit again.

To set up who approves:
1. In **HR → Settings → Access**, give roles the rights **Approve as department manager** and **Approve as department HR**.
2. In **HR → Settings → Resignations**, tick the departments each person looks after.

The full guide is **HR: Resignations and Approvals**.
