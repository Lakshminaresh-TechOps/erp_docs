# Employee Directory & Leave Management

The **HR & Team** module in VAP ERP helps company founders, HR managers, and department heads manage staff records, track daily attendance, process time-off requests, and maintain organizational clarity.

---

## 1. Adding Employees & Managing Profiles

1. Open the **App Launcher** and click **HR & Team**.
2. From the sidebar, click **Employees** and select **+ Add Employee**.
3. **Personal & Contact Information:**
   - Full Name, Personal Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth.
   - Emergency contact numbers and residential address.
4. **Employment Details:**
   - **Employee ID / Code:** Unique identifier (e.g. `EMP-0142`).
   - **Department:** Assign to a team (e.g. *Sales*, *Operations*, *Finance*, *Engineering*).
   - **Designation / Title:** Official role within the company.
   - **Reporting Manager:** Select the supervisor who approves leaves and reviews attendance.
   - **Joining Date:** Official first day of employment.
5. **System User Association:**
   - If the employee requires login access to VAP ERP, link their employee record to their user account with appropriate permissions.
6. Click **Save Employee**.

---

## 2. Tracking Daily Attendance

VAP ERP supports flexible attendance logging tailored to your workplace:
- **Web Check-In / Check-Out:** Employees log their start and finish times directly from their user dashboard.
- **Manager Daily Sheet:** Supervisors can view and adjust the daily attendance register (**Present**, **Absent**, **Half Day**, **On Duty**).
- **Monthly Summary:** Export monthly attendance sheets directly into the payroll calculation engine.

---

## 3. Leave Requests & Approvals

1. **Submitting a Leave Request (Employee):**
   - An employee logs into their portal and goes to **My Workspace → Request Leave**.
   - Selects the **Leave Type** (e.g. *Paid Leave*, *Sick Leave*, *Casual Leave*).
   - Chooses the start date, end date, and enters a brief explanation.
   - Clicks **Submit**.
2. **Reviewing Requests (Manager):**
   - The reporting manager receives an immediate notification.
   - Navigates to **HR → Leave Approvals** to review the request alongside the employee's remaining leave balance.
   - Clicks **Approve** or **Decline** (with an optional note).
   - The employee's leave balance updates automatically.

---

## 4. Organizing Departments & Cost Centers

Under **HR → Departments**, administrators can structure the company into functional units:
- Assign department heads.
- Track total headcounts per team.
- Group departmental operational expenses for clean financial reporting in the Finance module.
