# Admin: Organisation Details and Corrections

Your organisation's details live in **Admin → Organisation**. Once you have confirmed them during onboarding, the **legal details are locked**, so that nobody can change who your business legally is by editing a form.

---

## 1. What Is Locked and What Is Not

| Locked once confirmed | Still editable |
|-----------------------|----------------|
| Legal name | Trade name |
| Business structure | Logo and brand colour |
| GSTIN | Contact emails and phone numbers |
| PAN | Website, industry, headcount |
| CIN | Financial year, currency, time zone, date format |
| MSME registration number | Work locations |
| Tax residency | Invoice footer and authorised signatory |
| Registered address | |

Locked fields are shown read-only. If you try to save a change to them, you see: *"The legal details are confirmed and can only be changed with Correct business details."*

You can still check your GSTIN again with the verify button; that only marks the same GSTIN as verified.

---

## 2. Asking for a Correction

Use this for a mistake or a change of the **same** business, for example a typo in the legal name, a GSTIN typed wrongly at sign-up, or a registered office that moved.

1. Open **Admin → Organisation**. At the top, the box **Legal details are confirmed** has a button **Correct business details**.
2. Change only what is wrong. Everything else stays as it is.
3. Under **Why do these details need correcting?**, explain the change, for example *"Our registered office moved to Mumbai."*
4. Click **Send for review**.

You need the right to update settings. You can have one correction waiting at a time.

---

## 3. What Happens Next

The VAP ERP team reviews the request. They check the reason and, for a new GSTIN, that it is valid.

| Outcome | What you see |
|---------|--------------|
| **Approved** | The new details apply at once. The previous details are kept as history. A changed GSTIN shows as not verified until you verify it again. |
| **More information needed** | The box shows the team's note. Click **Change and send again**. |
| **Not approved** | The box shows the reason. You can send a new request. |

While a request is waiting, you can click **Cancel request**.

---

## 4. When the Business Itself Changes Hands

If the business has been sold or moved to a new legal entity, with a new owner, that is **not a correction**. Contact VAP ERP support about changing the ownership of the organisation. A correction request for a sale will not be approved.

---

## 5. Invoices Keep Their Details

Every invoice from VAP ERP keeps the buyer details (legal name, trade name, GSTIN and address) **as they were on the day it was issued**. When your details change later, by a correction or otherwise, your earlier invoices do not change. Only new invoices show the new details.

---

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**"The legal name fields are greyed out."**
Your details are confirmed. Use **Correct business details**.

**"I only want to change our trade name or logo."**
You can change those directly. They are not locked.

**"Our details are not confirmed yet."**
Then you can still edit everything directly. They lock when you confirm them.
