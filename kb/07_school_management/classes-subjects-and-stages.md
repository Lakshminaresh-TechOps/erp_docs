# Classes, Sections, Subjects and Stages

The **Academics** page holds the foundation of the school: classes with their sections, the subjects you teach, and rooms. It has three tabs: **Classes**, **Subjects** and **Rooms**.

---

## What is a stage and why does it matter?

Every class belongs to one of three stages:

- **Play school**: nursery, LKG, UKG and similar.
- **School**: Class 1 to 10.
- **Intermediate**: Class 11 and 12.

Subjects are taught in one or more stages. A subject with no stage ticked is offered to every stage. Because of this, a School class is only offered school subjects, and an Intermediate class is offered intermediate subjects. This keeps lists short and stops, for example, a physics paper from appearing for a Class 5.

---

## How do I add a class?

1. Open **Academics** and stay on the **Classes** tab.
2. Click **Add class**.
3. Type the class name (for example Class 6), choose its stage, and click **Save**.

You can filter the class list by stage with the chips above the list. Each class shows a stage badge. Classes created before stages existed show a suggested stage worked out from the name, such as "Intermediate 1" or "LKG". The suggestion is shown dashed and is only saved when you pick a stage yourself.

To change a stage later, open the class and use the **Stage** choices.

---

## How do I add sections?

Open a class and, under **Sections**, click **Add section** and type a name such as A. Add as many as the class has.

---

## How do I add subjects and say where they are taught?

1. Open the **Subjects** tab and click **Add subject**.
2. Type the name and choose **Taught in**: **All stages**, **Play school**, **School** or **Intermediate**.
3. In the list, the **Taught in** column has one button per stage. Click a button to switch that stage on or off for the subject. With none on, the column reads "All stages".

---

## How do I choose the subjects a class studies this year?

Open the class. Under **Subjects this year**, tick the subjects the class studies. Only subjects that fit the class's stage are listed. If you need one from another stage, use **Show N subjects of other stages**. Subjects already linked to the class are always shown.

If a class has no subjects chosen yet, the timetable offers the subjects of its stage instead of every subject in the school.

---

## How do I set the class teacher and subject teachers?

Open the class. **Class teacher** lets you pick the teacher in charge of the class. **Subject teachers** lets you pick a teacher for each subject of that class. These choices are used first when the timetable is filled in.

---

## What are rooms for?

The **Rooms** tab keeps a simple list of rooms, so they can be named in the school's records.
