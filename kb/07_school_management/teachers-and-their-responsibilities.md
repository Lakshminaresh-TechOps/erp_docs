# Teachers and Their Responsibilities

The **Teachers** page, titled **Teachers & Responsibilities**, lists the teacher profiles of the school and records what each teacher is responsible for.

---

## How do I add a teacher?

1. Open the **School** module and click **Teachers**.
2. Click to add a teacher. The **Add teacher** form opens.
3. Fill in the name and contact details and save.

If the teacher should sign in, give them access from the school's access settings so that they can use the phone app and the web pages that suit their work.

---

## What are the kinds of responsibility?

Open a teacher and look under **Who teaches what**. A teacher can be:

- **Class teacher**: in charge of a whole class.
- **Section teacher**: in charge of one section of a class (for example 6 B).
- **Subject teacher**: teaches one subject to a class. Subject teachers are set per class in **Academics** under **Subject teachers**.

The list **Now responsible for** shows what the teacher holds today.

---

## How do I make someone a class teacher or section teacher?

1. Open the teacher, then go to **Who teaches what**.
2. Under **Add**, choose the **Role** (Class teacher or Section teacher), the **Class** and, for a section teacher, the **Section**.
3. Set **Starts on** and save.

A class or a section has one such teacher at a time. If you try to add a second, the page explains that this seat already has a teacher.

To end a responsibility, click **Remove** next to it.

---

## Why does a teacher see "No classes to mark"?

On the phone, a teacher marks attendance for the classes and sections they are responsible for. A teacher with no responsibility yet sees "No classes to mark". Give them a responsibility as described above and the classes appear.

---

## Where is the class teacher shown?

In the **Class timetable**, the class teacher of the class (or of the section being shown) is named at the top. The timetable also tells you when the class teacher has no period with the class that week.
