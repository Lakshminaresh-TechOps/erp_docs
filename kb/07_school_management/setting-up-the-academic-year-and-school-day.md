# Setting Up the Academic Year and the School Day

Before classes, fees or a timetable can work, the school needs two things in **School Settings**: a current academic year and a school day (the bell schedule). This guide shows both. The settings page is split into tabs: **Academic year**, **School day**, **Student documents**, **Access** and **Inventory store**.

---

## How do I set the current academic year?

1. Open the **School** module and click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Stay on the **Academic year** tab.
3. Add the session if it is not listed yet (for example 2026-27) with its start and end dates.
4. Click **Make current** next to the session you are running now.

Once a session is current, fees and the timetable use it. If you see a message such as "Set a current academic year in School Settings first", this step has not been done yet.

---

## How do I set up the school day?

1. Open **Settings** and click the **School day** tab.
2. Under **Set up the school day**, fill in how a normal day runs:
   - **First bell**: the time the first period starts.
   - **Minutes in a period**: how long each period lasts.
   - **Periods in the day**: how many periods there are.
3. Under **Breaks**, click **Add break** for each break. For every break give a **Break name** (for example Lunch), **After period** (which period it follows) and **Minutes** (how long it lasts). Use **Remove** to delete a break you do not need.
4. Check the preview under **What it will look like**. It lists every period and break with its clock time.
5. Under **Apply to**, tick the days that run this way and click **Apply to N days**.

The periods and breaks are created for you. You do not type each time by hand.

---

## What if one day runs differently?

Use **Change one day** on the same tab. Pick the day, then move a time, take a period out, or add an extra one such as PT. Other days are not affected.

---

## Why does the timetable show no periods?

The timetable is built on the school day. If the **Timetable** page is empty, the school day has not been applied yet. Complete the steps above, then return to **Timetable**.

---

## Who can change these settings?

School Settings are managed by the school's administrator. Teachers and other staff only see the result in the timetable.
