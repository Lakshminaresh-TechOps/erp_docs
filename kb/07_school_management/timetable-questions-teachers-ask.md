# Timetable Questions Teachers Ask

---

## How do I see my own timetable?

Open **Timetable** in the School module and click **Teacher timetable**. Choose your name. Your week is shown with the class, section and subject for each period.

---

## Why is a period I expected not in my timetable?

A period appears only after the school's administrator has planned it. If it is missing, ask them to open **Class timetable**, choose the class and add you to that period. They can also use auto-fill to plan a whole class.

---

## Why do I see a clash message?

The timetable does not allow one teacher to take two classes in the same period. If you are asked to teach a class in a period where you already teach another, the planner says that you already teach that class on that day and period. The administrator can pick another free teacher or change the other period.

---

## Why can I not mark attendance for a class?

You can mark attendance for the classes and sections you are responsible for. If the phone shows "No classes to mark", you have not been made a class teacher or section teacher yet. Ask the administrator to add the responsibility in **Teachers**, under **Who teaches what**.

---

## Who changes a period, a break or the school day?

The school's administrator. Breaks and period times come from **Settings**, under the **School day** tab. Teachers see the result in the timetable but do not edit it.

---

## How do I know which class I am the class teacher of?

Open **Timetable**, choose the class and look at the line **Class teacher** at the top. Your name is shown there when you hold that responsibility.
