# Creating & Sending Tax Invoices

The **Finance & Invoicing** module in VAP ERP allows you to issue professional, tax-compliant invoices, track customer payment statuses, and maintain a clear ledger of accounts receivable.

---

## 1. Creating a New Invoice

1. Open the **App Launcher** (grid icon in top navigation) and click **Finance**.
2. From the left sidebar, click **Invoices** and select **+ New Invoice**.
3. **Select or Add a Customer:**
   - Choose an existing client from your customer directory, or click **+ Add Customer** to enter their business name, contact email, and billing address.
   - If applicable, enter the customer's Tax / GST Identification Number.
4. **Set Invoice Dates:**
   - **Invoice Date:** The date the transaction took place (defaults to today).
   - **Payment Terms:** Select payment terms (e.g. *Due upon Receipt*, *Net 15*, *Net 30*). The system will calculate the due date automatically.
5. **Add Line Items:**
   - Click **+ Add Item** and select products or services from your catalog, or type a custom description.
   - Enter the **Quantity** and **Unit Price**.
   - Select the applicable tax rate (e.g. GST 5%, 12%, 18%, or Tax Exempt). The system calculates line-level and total taxes automatically.
6. **Add Notes & Payment Details:**
   - Enter bank transfer details, UPI IDs, or special terms and conditions in the **Customer Notes** box.
7. **Save or Issue:**
   - Click **Save as Draft** if you want to review it later without affecting your sales ledger.
   - Click **Issue Invoice** to finalize the invoice number and update your customer's outstanding balance.

---

## 2. Sending the Invoice & Exporting PDFs

Once an invoice is issued, you can:
- **Download PDF:** Click **Export PDF** to generate a clean, branded document featuring your company logo, payment details, and tax breakdowns.
- **Send via Email:** Click **Send Email** to dispatch the invoice directly to the customer's billing email with the PDF attached.
- **Copy Payment Link:** Share a direct web link with the customer to view their invoice summary online.

---

## 3. Recording Payments

When a customer pays an invoice:
1. Open the invoice from the **Invoices** list.
2. Click **Record Payment** in the top action bar.
3. Enter the **Amount Received** (supports partial payments if the client paid an installment).
4. Select the **Payment Date** and **Payment Mode** (e.g. *Bank Transfer / NEFT*, *Cheque*, *Cash*, or *Online Payment*).
5. Enter any reference or transaction ID.
6. Click **Save Payment**. The invoice status updates automatically to **Paid** (or **Partially Paid**), and your customer's outstanding balance decreases accordingly.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting & Tips

- **How do I correct a mistake on an issued invoice?**  
  To maintain accounting integrity, issued invoices cannot be arbitrarily edited once sent. If you need to make an adjustment, either issue a **Credit Note** to offset the difference or cancel the invoice and generate a revised one.
- **Can I set up recurring invoices for monthly retainers?**  
  Yes. Navigate to **Finance → Recurring Invoices** to configure automatic monthly or quarterly billing schedules for retainer clients.
