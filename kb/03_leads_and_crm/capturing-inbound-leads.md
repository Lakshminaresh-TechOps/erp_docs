# Capturing & Managing Inbound Leads

The **Leads & CRM** module in VAP ERP helps your sales team centralize incoming customer inquiries, track conversations, organize deals on a visual board, and turn prospects into long-term clients.

---

## 1. How Inbound Leads Enter Your System

VAP ERP provides three ways to bring prospects into your CRM:
1. **Manual Entry:** Click **+ New Lead** in the CRM dashboard to enter contact details, expected deal value, and source (e.g. *Referral*, *Phone Call*, *Trade Show*).
2. **Bulk Spreadsheet Import:** Upload a clean CSV or Excel sheet under **CRM → Import Leads** to ingest historical customer contacts.
3. **Automated Webhooks:** Connect your website contact forms, Meta lead ads, or landing pages directly to your VAP ERP webhook URL so new inquiries appear in your pipeline within seconds.

---

## 2. Managing Your Visual Sales Pipeline

Navigate to **CRM → Pipeline** to view your active opportunities on a Kanban board organized by sales stages:

- **New:** Inquiries just received that require initial qualification.
- **Contacted:** Sales reps have initiated outreach via phone, email, or WhatsApp.
- **Meeting / Demo Scheduled:** Prospects evaluating product fit.
- **Proposal Sent:** Quotations or commercial terms issued.
- **Won:** Successfully closed deals.
- **Lost:** Inactive or disqualified leads.

### Quick Actions on the Board:
- **Drag & Drop:** Simply drag a deal card across columns as negotiations progress.
- **Activity Logging:** Click any lead card to log phone call summaries, schedule follow-up reminders, or record meeting notes.
- **Team Assignment:** Assign leads to specific sales executives to balance workloads and ensure fast response times.

---

## 3. Converting a Won Lead into a Customer

When a prospect accepts your quotation and is ready to do business:
1. Open the lead detail view.
2. Click **Convert to Customer** in the top action bar.
3. The system automatically:
   - Creates a permanent record in your **Customer Directory**.
   - Preserves all historical sales notes, contact names, and phone numbers.
   - Enables immediate generation of sales orders and tax invoices under the **Finance** module without re-typing any customer information.

---

## 4. Best Practices for High Sales Conversion

- **Respond Within 15 Minutes:** Use automated email notifications to alert sales reps the moment a new inbound lead is captured.
- **Set Next Steps on Every Call:** Never leave a lead card without an upcoming task or scheduled follow-up date.
- **Track Lost Reasons:** When marking a lead as *Lost*, select a reason (e.g. *Pricing*, *Competitor*, *Timeline*). This data helps your management team refine commercial packaging over time.
