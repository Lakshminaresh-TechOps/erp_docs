# Recruit: Job Openings and Your Public Careers Page

Every hire starts with an **opening**. Published openings appear on your organisation's public **careers page**, where anyone can apply without signing in.

---

## 1. Creating an Opening

1. Open **Recruit → Openings** and click **New opening**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Job title**, for example *Backend engineer* or *Mathematics teacher*.
   - **Department** and **Location**, chosen from your organisation's departments and locations.
   - **Employment type:** Full time, Part time, Contract, Internship or Temporary.
   - **How many people** you want to hire for this opening.
   - **Description** of the role.
   - **Show on the careers page until (optional):** the last day the opening is listed.
   - **Interview panel:** the staff who will interview applicants for this opening.
3. Save. A new opening starts as a **Draft**, visible only inside Recruit.

---

## 2. Opening Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Next actions |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Draft** | Being prepared; not on the careers page | Publish, Close |
| **Published** | Listed on the careers page; applicants can apply | Pause, Close |
| **Paused** | Hidden from the careers page for now; existing applicants stay | Publish, Close |
| **Closed** | Finished; no new applications | None |

When an applicant accepts an offer and the opening has hired as many people as it needed, the opening is closed automatically.

---

## 3. Your Public Careers Page

Set it up in **Recruit → Settings → Careers page**.

- **Your public careers page** box shows the address, with **Copy link**, **Open as applicant** and a **QR code** you can download for posters and print adverts.
- **Show the careers page:** switch the page on or off. While it is off, the address shows "not available" and nothing about your organisation.
- **Organisation details:** the page uses your organisation's name, logo, brand colour, industry, city and website from **Admin → Organisation**, so they are the same everywhere. Change them there.
- **Careers page content:** a headline, an introduction, *About us*, up to 8 *Why join us* reasons, up to 6 *How we hire* steps, up to 10 *Questions applicants ask*, and an optional contact email for applicants.
- **Let search engines list the careers page:** off by default, so only people with the link find it.
- **Preview** shows the page with your unsaved changes and your published openings.

Click **Save settings** to apply your changes. Put a "Careers" link to the address on your website.

---

## 4. How Applicants Apply

1. The applicant opens your careers page and chooses an opening.
2. They fill in their name, email and phone, and attach their CV (PDF or Word, smaller than 5 MB).
3. They agree to how long their application is kept, and send it.
4. They see "Thank you, your application is in". The application arrives in the opening's **Applied** stage.

The careers page shows no ERP screens and no sign-in; it is only for applying.
