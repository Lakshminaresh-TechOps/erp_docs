# Recruit: Offer Acceptance and HR Joining Soon

Once an offer is sent, the applicant answers it online through their private link. An accepted offer flows into **HR → Joining soon**, where HR prepares the new person's first day.

---

## 1. What the Applicant Sees

The offer link opens a page with your organisation's logo and name showing:

- **Offer of employment** for the applicant, with the full offer letter.
- A button to download the letter as a PDF.
- The date by which they should answer.

No sign-in is needed. The link is private to the applicant; anyone without it sees nothing.

---

## 2. Accepting the Offer

1. The applicant types their full name as it appears on the offer.
2. They tick "I have read the offer letter and I accept this offer".
3. They click **Accept offer**.

Their name, the time, their internet address and the letter's fingerprint are recorded as the acceptance. After acceptance the offer can no longer be changed.

In Recruit, straight after acceptance:
- The offer shows **Accepted**.
- The applicant moves to **Hired** by themselves.
- If the opening has now hired everyone it needed, it is closed.

---

## 3. Declining the Offer

The applicant clicks **I want to decline**, may give a reason, and confirms. The offer shows **Declined** with the reason. Declining cannot be undone; if the applicant changes their mind, draft a new offer.

---

## 4. Expired Offers

If the applicant does not answer before the link closes, the offer shows **Expired** and the page tells the applicant to contact your organisation. To give them more time, click **New link** on the offer in Recruit and send the new link.

---

## 5. HR → Joining Soon

Everyone who accepts an offer appears in **HR → Joining soon** by themselves, with their designation, department, location, joining date, contact details and offered pay.

For each person, HR can:

1. **Add to HR people:** creates their HR record so their login can be requested and onboarding tasks start.
2. **Mark as joined** on their first day.
3. **Not joining** if they do not join after all.

HR can also click **Add someone** to add a person who was hired outside Recruit.
