# Recruit: Interviews and Scorecards

Interviews in Recruit are organised in **rounds**. Each round has a panel of interviewers, and each interviewer fills in their own **scorecard**.

---

## 1. Scheduling an Interview

1. Open the applicant's page and click **Schedule an interview**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Round**, for example *First round* or *Technical interview*.
   - **Date and time** and **Length**.
   - **How:** Video call, In person or Phone.
   - **Meeting link (optional)** for video calls.
   - **Panel:** pick the interviewers from your staff.
3. Save. The applicant moves to the **Interview** stage by themselves.
4. Click **Calendar file** to download the invitation and send it to the applicant and the panel. Invitation emails will come in a later update.

To move or change an interview, open it and click **Change interview**. To call it off, cancel it with a reason.

---

## 2. Interview Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Scheduled** | Booked and coming up |
| **Done** | The interview took place (marked with **Mark as done**) |
| **Cancelled** | Called off; the reason is in the history |

---

## 3. My Interviews (for Interviewers)

Every panel member sees **Recruit → My interviews**, even if they have no other Recruit access. It lists the interviews they are on, upcoming first. Opening one shows the applicant's CV and the round details, and the scorecard.

---

## 4. Filling In a Scorecard

1. Open the interview from **My interviews**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Overall rating**.
   - **Recommendation:** Strong yes, Yes, No or Strong no.
   - **Strengths** and **Concerns**.
3. Click **Save draft** to come back later, or **Submit scorecard** when you are done.

To keep scoring fair, other panel members do not see your scorecard until they have submitted their own. Once submitted, a scorecard cannot be changed.

---

## 5. Scorecards Before an Offer

In **Recruit → Settings → Interviews**, **Need scorecards before an offer** is on by default. While it is on, an applicant can be moved to an Offer or Hired stage only when every interview marked as done has at least one submitted scorecard. Switch it off if your organisation does not use scorecards.
