# Recruit: Applicants and Hiring Stages

Each opening has its own board of applicants, arranged by **hiring stage**. Moving an applicant from stage to stage records who did it and when.

---

## 1. Seeing an Opening's Applicants

1. Open **Recruit → Openings** and click the opening.
2. The board shows one column per stage, with a card for each applicant.
3. Click an applicant to open their page: contact details, CV, stage, interviews, offer and history.

---

## 2. What Each Stage Means

Every stage has one of six meanings. You can rename stages and add your own, but each one keeps a meaning so Recruit knows what to do.

| Meaning | Used for |
|---------|----------|
| **Applied** | New applications arrive here |
| **Screening** | Checking CVs and first calls |
| **Interview** | Interview rounds |
| **Offer** | An offer is being prepared or is out |
| **Hired** | Accepted and joining |
| **Rejected** | Not taken forward (needs a reason) |

---

## 3. Moving an Applicant

- On the applicant's page, choose the new stage and confirm.
- Some moves happen by themselves:
  - Scheduling an interview moves the applicant to **Interview**.
  - Sending an offer moves them to **Offer**.
  - Accepting the offer moves them to **Hired**.
- If **Need scorecards before an offer** is switched on in Settings, moving someone to an Offer or Hired stage needs at least one submitted scorecard for every interview marked as done.

---

## 4. Rejecting an Applicant

Moving an applicant to a **Rejected** stage asks for a reason:

- Not a fit for the role
- Skills do not match
- Not enough experience
- Salary expectations
- Location or travel
- Applicant withdrew
- No response from applicant
- Position filled
- Other (add a note)

The reason is kept in the applicant's history for reporting and fairness.

---

## 5. The Applicant's History

The history on each applicant's page lists everything that happened, in order: stage moves (and whether they were automatic), interviews scheduled or cancelled, offers drafted, approved, sent back, sent, accepted, declined or withdrawn.

---

## 6. Setting Up Your Stages

Open **Recruit → Settings → Stages** to rename stages, add new ones (for example *Technical test* with the meaning *Screening*), reorder them, or switch off ones you do not use. Stages save on their own with the button on that tab. Recruit tells you what is wrong if a change would break a stage that is in use.

---

## 7. How Long Applications Are Kept

In **Recruit → Settings → Keeping applications**, choose how many months the applications of people you did not hire are kept (1 to 60 months). Applicants agree to this period when they apply, so changing it also changes the wording new applicants see.
