# Recruit: Overview, Menus and Team Access

**Recruit** is the hiring module of VAP ERP. It takes a person from a job opening on your public careers page, through screening and interviews, to a signed offer, and then hands them to **HR → Joining soon** so their first day is ready.

---

## 1. The Hiring Journey at a Glance

1. **Openings:** create a job opening and publish it.
2. **Careers page:** applicants find the opening on your public careers page and apply with their CV.
3. **Applicants:** move each applicant through your hiring stages (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired or Rejected).
4. **Interviews:** schedule interview rounds with a panel; each panel member fills in a scorecard.
5. **Offers:** draft an offer from your HR salary bands, get it approved, and send the applicant a private offer link.
6. **Acceptance:** the applicant accepts online; they move to **Hired** automatically and appear in **HR → Joining soon**.

---

## 2. The Recruit Menus

| Menu | What it is for | Who sees it |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Overview** | A summary of openings and applicants | Everyone with Recruit access |
| **Openings** | Job openings and, inside each one, its applicants | People who can read openings or applicants |
| **My interviews** | The interviews you are on the panel for, with your scorecards | Everyone with Recruit access, and interview panel members |
| **Offers** | All offers, and the ones waiting for your approval | People who can read offers or approve them |
| **Settings** | Careers page, stages, interviews, offers and how long applications are kept | People who can read Recruit settings |
| **Team** | Recruit roles and who holds them | Organisation admins only |

You only see the menus your role allows. If a menu is missing, ask your organisation admin to check your Recruit role in **Recruit → Team**.

---

## 3. Who Can Use Recruit

- **Organisation users** (staff with an organisation login) can be given Recruit roles.
- **Module users** (people who sign in to a single module, such as a school-only login) cannot hold Recruit roles or approve offers. If one of them needs Recruit, an admin first makes them an organisation user in **Admin**.
- **Interview panel members** who have no Recruit role still see **My interviews**, so they can run their interview and submit a scorecard. They see nothing else in Recruit.
- **Organisation admins** can do everything in Recruit by default.

---

## 4. Giving People Recruit Roles (Recruit → Team)

Only an organisation admin opens **Recruit → Team**. The first time it opens, four starter roles are created:

| Starter role | What it can do |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Recruiter** | Runs openings, moves applicants, schedules interviews, drafts offers, reads settings |
| **Hiring manager** | Sees openings and applicants, runs interviews, reads offers |
| **Department manager** | Sees openings and applicants, and approves offers for the departments they look after |
| **Offer approver** | Sees openings and applicants, and gives the final approval of offers |

To give someone access:

1. Open **Recruit → Team**.
2. Find the person by name, email or staff number.
3. Choose one or more Recruit roles for them and save. Changes apply straight away.

To make your own role, use **New role**, give it a name, and tick what it can do (for example *Read offers* or *Approve offers*).

### Departments a manager looks after

For approval steps that belong to a department (for example "the SMS department manager checks the budget"), click **Departments** next to a person and tick the departments they look after. Only people who hold the step's role **and** look after the offer's department can approve that step.

---

## 5. Organisation Admins and Offer Approvals

Organisation admins approve offers by default, at any step. If an admin should not receive approvals (for example an IT admin), open **Recruit → Team** and click **Stop approving** on their row. Click **Approve offers** to switch it back on. The badge on the row always shows which way it is set.

Two rules apply to everyone, admins included:
- Nobody approves an offer they drafted.
- One person approves only one step of the same offer.
