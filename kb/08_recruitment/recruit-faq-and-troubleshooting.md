# Recruit: Questions and Troubleshooting

---

## Access and Menus

**I cannot see Recruit, or a menu is missing.**
You only see what your Recruit role allows. Ask your organisation admin to give you a role in **Recruit → Team**. If you are on an interview panel only, you see just **My interviews**.

**I cannot find Recruit → Team.**
Only organisation admins see **Team**.

**Someone does not appear in Recruit → Team.**
Team lists active organisation users only. A module user (one-module login) must first be made an organisation user in **Admin**.

## Approvals

**Why can I not approve this offer?**
The offer page tells you why. The usual reasons:
- You drafted it. Someone else must approve it.
- You already approved an earlier step of the same offer.
- The step needs a different Recruit role.
- The step is for the offer's department, and you do not look after that department.
- You are an organisation admin who has been switched off from approving.

**Sending for approval says "No one can approve" a step.**
Nobody other than the person who drafted the offer can approve that step yet. In **Recruit → Team**, give someone the step's role (and, for a department step, tick the offer's department under **Departments**), or let an organisation admin approve.

**It says "Choose the offer's department first".**
A step is limited to the opening's department, but the offer has no department. Edit the offer and choose its department.

**An approver should not get approvals.**
Remove their approving role in **Recruit → Team**. For an organisation admin, click **Stop approving**.

**Can we skip approval?**
Yes. Remove all steps in **Recruit → Settings → Offers**. Offers can then be sent straight from the draft.

**We changed an approved offer and it went back to Draft.**
That is by design: any change after approval needs approval again, from the first step.

## Offer Links

**The applicant lost the link, or the offer expired.**
Open the offer and click **New link**, then send the new link. The old link stops working.

**The applicant says the link does not open.**
Check the offer is still **Sent**. Withdrawn, declined, expired and replaced links no longer open.

**How long does an offer link stay open?**
7 days unless you change **Offer link stays open for (days)** in **Recruit → Settings → Offers** (1 to 60 days).

**Where is the signed acceptance?**
On the offer: the applicant's typed name, the time and the letter's fingerprint are kept with it.

## Careers Page and Applications

**The careers page says "not available".**
Switch on **Show the careers page** in **Recruit → Settings → Careers page** and save.

**An opening is not on the careers page.**
Only **Published** openings are listed. Paused, draft and closed openings are hidden, as are openings past their *Show on the careers page until* date.

**The logo or company name on the careers page is wrong.**
Change it in **Admin → Organisation**; the careers page always uses those details.

**An applicant cannot upload their CV.**
The CV must be a PDF or Word file (DOC or DOCX) smaller than 5 MB.

## Interviews and Stages

**I cannot move an applicant to Offer or Hired.**
**Need scorecards before an offer** is on, and an interview marked as done has no submitted scorecard yet. Ask the panel to submit their scorecards, or switch the rule off in **Recruit → Settings → Interviews**.

**I cannot see another interviewer's scorecard.**
Submit your own first. Scorecards stay hidden from other panel members until they have submitted theirs.

**Do applicants receive interview emails?**
Not yet. Download the **Calendar file** from the interview and send it to the applicant and the panel.
